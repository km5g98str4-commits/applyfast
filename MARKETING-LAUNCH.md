## 📝 ProductHunt Listing (ApplyFast)

### Tagline
"Apply to jobs in 30 seconds — AI that reads the job, analyzes your CV, and writes the perfect tailored application. Built for speed."

### Description
I spent 3 hours applying to 5 jobs last month. Most of it was copy-pasting, tweaking cover letters, and trying to sound like the "ideal candidate" for each role.

So I built ApplyFast.

**What it does:**
1. Paste any job URL → it auto-fetches and analyzes the JD
2. Upload your CV → AI extracts everything
3. Choose your sector/template → get a perfectly tailored application
4. ATS analysis shows you exactly how well you match

**Why it's different:**
- 🇸🇦 Arabic-first (built for MENA market, but works in English too)
- 🚫 No browser automation (zero ToS risks)
- 🧠 DeepSeek AI as primary (faster + cheaper), OpenAI fallback
- 🎯 Sector-specific templates (tech, healthcare, finance, education...)
- 📊 Built-in ATS compatibility scoring

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16, DeepSeek API, OpenAI fallback, Redis, Lemon Squeezy

**Free tier:** 3 applications/day. No sign-up needed.

### Launch Offer for ProductHunt Community
🎉 First 50 PH users get 10 free applications. Just mention "PH" in your first use.
